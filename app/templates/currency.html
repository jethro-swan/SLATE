manage currency

This page will be visible only to the stewards of this currency. It will show the following:

  • A link to display a list of all accounts in this currency, along with the owner and current balance of each.

  • A link to download a CSV list of all accounts in this currency, along with the owner and current balance of each.

  • A link to export (download) a CSV list of all payments made in this currency, showing
    • date and time
    • payment number (sequential)
    • payer account
    • payer identity
    • payee account
    • payee identity
    • amount
    • annotation

    e.g.

    download payments list

    The filename will be generated automatically comprising

    • "currency_"
    • the FPH of the **currency**
    • the date and time (in bigendian format)
    and a download link will be created.

    It's not yet obvious how to implement this. The download link must restrict access to this steward (the one downloading the file) and should be time-limited.


  • A link to create a pop-out window wide enough to list of all payments made in this currency, showing
    • date and time
    • payment number (sequential)
    • payer account
    • payer identity
    • payee account
    • payee identity
    • amount
    • annotation

  • A link to a form to add or recommend a new steward for this currency. The latter seems more likely because all current stewards should probably have an opportunity to veto or otherwise decide upon the new steward. This is a policy matter we need to discuss.

  • A link to a form to propose removal of an existing steward of this currency. All current stewards should probably have an opportunity to veto or otherwise decide upon the matter, including the steward whose removal is being proposed. This is a policy matter we need to discuss.
