## SLATE screen templates

The templates here have been heavily modified (reduced in both number and
sophistication) from those used in the _NESTS SWI_.

For the time being, those not used have been moved to a subdirectory named
"probably_not_needed".

The following are being renamed for consistency, initially by hard-linking:

	agent_create.html	>	create_agent.html
	currency_create.html   	>       create_currency.html
	namespace_create.html   >       create_namespace.html

	agent_manage.html	>	manage_agent.html
	currency_manage.html    >       manage_currency.html
	namespace_manage.html   >       manage_namespace.html
