The active page replacing this in due course will contain:

  • a link to create new currency
  • a list of the existing currencies over which this identity shares stewardship (not necessarily as its original creator), including links to:
    • status [add1|suspend2|remove2]
    • policy 3 [permissive|merchant|restrictive]
    • stewards [add4|suspend5|remove5]
    • export a JSON dump of all transactions6 in this currency
    • import JSON record of all transactions7 in this currency

NOTES:

  1. Any identity may create a new currency if, and according to conditions defined by the stewards of its parent namespace. If authorized, that identity becomes the first stewards of that new currency.
  2. The conditions allowing the stewards of any currency to suspend or remove (i.e. archive) it are defined by the stewards of its parent namespace.
    • If permissive, any identity may create an account in this currency immediately.
    • If merchant, any identity may create an account in this currency immediately if invited to do so by an identity registered as a merchant in this currency.
    • If restrictive, any identity may apply to create an account in this currency but must wait for its authorization by the stewards of the currency..
    However it is created, any account may subsequently be suspended (or, exceptionally, subjected to more permanent restrictions) by the stewards of the currency.
  4. The conditions allowing the stewards of any currency to add a new steward or to remove an existing steward are defined by the stewards of its parent namespace.
  5. Unless the policy set by the stewards of its parent namespace permits it (according to some majority vote criterion) the removal of a currency's steward will require referral to the stewards of its parent namespace. The policy may permit more immediate suspension, the suspended steward having the right to appeal to stewards of its parent namespace.
  6. An of the stewards of a currency may export a JSON dump of all transactions within that currency. The accounts will be identified by both HRNS (the full namespace path) and FPH (the 64-character ID).
  7. Where permitted by the policy defined by the stewards of its parent namespace, a steward of a newly created currency may import a JSON listing of accounts (identified by HRNS) and transactions between them.
    Since this is a new currency, there will be no existing accounts but some of the identities may exist already. Therefore, if any account-holder listed exists already, the corresponding account will be ignored unless the policy configuration (set by both the stewards) and by the individual identity will have to allow the account's creation.
    Any non-existing account-holders will be created (along with any required new intermediate namespaces) for which reason the JSON listing will will have to include some initial access authorization details).
    Such an import will generally be use to permit demonstrations, simulations, etc., and it will not be possible to overwrite or extend a "live" currency's account set.
