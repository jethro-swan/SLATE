Make a payment

{% if hub_mode == "slate_simple" %}

You can make a payment to any account if

  • you know its identifier (either its HRNS or its FPH); and
  • you have at least one account in the same currency.
If you have at least one account in the same currency, it will be displayed in the payment form. If you have more than one account in this currency a list will displayed from which you can select an account.

It is also possible to make a payment to a known identity rather than to an account. In this case, if you can specify the identity (either its HRNS or its FPH) of the payee, you will be shown a list of currencies in which you both have an account, along with the current balance of each.1, 2

The payment options are:

  • Pay to a known account (identified by HRNS or FPH)
  • Pay to a known identity identified by name 2
  • Pay to a known identity (identified by HRNS or FPH) 2

  1. These accounts may be in a variety of different currencies, so it is your responsibility to identify which, if any, is suitable for your need.
  2. Not yet implemented.
