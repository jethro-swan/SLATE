Payments

Click on any entry in the account-holder column to make a payment. The ◼ will change to ☐ to confirm the selection.

Transaction history

Click on ◼ in any row to display the transaction history for that currency:account-holder pairing or to export (as CSV) a more complete version.

Currency stewardship

If you are a steward of a currency it will be shown as a link on which you can click to manage it.

Here you can:

  • Access the complete transaction history (all payments and receipts made in this currency) and to export it as a CSV.
  • Add additional stewards.
  • Propose the removal of a steward.
  • De-activate the currency. This is generally an action of last resort, taken only when misuse of the currency has been reported to its stewards.
  • Re-activate the currency.

Accessing additional functions

Click on the + in the top right hand corner of the table to reveal links to further functions:

Creating a new namespace

Click on new namespace and enter a name along with a valid parent namespace (which must exist already and must be one in which you are authorized to create new entities - which will generally be the case with open namespaces but will generally not be the case with private namespaces). You will be the initial steward of this new namespace but can add others.

Creating a new currency

Click on new currency and enter a name along with a valid parent namespace (which must exist already and must be one in which you are authorized to create new entities - which will generally be the case with open namespaces but will generally not be the case with private namespaces). You will be the initial steward of this new currency but can add others.

Creating a QR code invitation

Clicking on invite user will allow you to create a QR code encoding
  • The URL of this instance of SLATE.
  • The initial currency to be used by the new user.
  • The initial namespace to be used by the new user.
This enables the rapid reccuitment of others with whom you can begin immediate interaction.

Registration

To register, enter the following details:

  • A short username comprising alphanumeric characters. The first character must be a letter.
  • The identifier of an existing namespace.
  • The identifier of an existing currency.
  • At least one email address to allow recovery of login credentials. A cryptographic hash of the email address(es) will be saved to allow validation of the address provide in the password/PIN reset request, but the address itself will not be stored.
  • A password (something random-looking and unguessable).
  • A 6-digit PIN.
If you have reached this form by following an invitation link (typically by scanning a QR code), the namespace or currency field, usually both, will have been replaced by their identifiers.

Login recovery

If you have forgotten your password or PIN, you can create new login credentials by clicking on Forgotten password or PIN? and entering your login identity (as created when you registered) along with one of the email addresses you provided for recovery purposes. If that address is valid, you will be sent a one-time recovery link taking you to a form in which you can enter a new passsword and PIN.

General

This is an experimental prototype intended to meet the following needs:

  • Demonstration of open money and open measure principles.
  • A self-learning tool.
  • A modelling, simulation and visualization tool - including a foundation for agent-based modelling.
  • A prototype and reference implementation ("executable documentation") for the development of a functional equivalents which might be more easily deployed, more scalable and more efficient.
  • A shorter-term solution for real world deployment.
